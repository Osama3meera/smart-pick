You brush, you use mouthwash, you chew mint after mint… and an hour later, the bad breath is back.

You are not alone. Most bad breath does not come from your stomach. It starts **in your mouth**, and that is good news, because it means simple daily habits can fix most of it. Below are 7 natural ways to get fresher breath, followed by an honest look at whether a probiotic like **ProDentim** is worth adding.

## What causes bad breath?

In most people, bad breath comes from **bacteria in the mouth**, especially on the back of the tongue and between the teeth. As they break down food bits, they release smelly sulfur gases.

Things that make it worse:

- A **dry mouth**, because saliva is your natural cleaner
- Food stuck **between your teeth**
- **Gum problems** and cavities
- Smoking, coffee, alcohol, and strong foods like garlic and onion

## 7 ways to get rid of bad breath naturally

1. **Clean your tongue every day.** Most odour-causing bacteria live on the back of the tongue. A cheap tongue scraper or your toothbrush makes a big difference.
2. **Floss every day.** Food trapped between your teeth breaks down and smells. Flossing removes it before it does.
3. **Drink more water.** A dry mouth is a smelly mouth. Sip water through the day, especially after coffee.
4. **Chew sugar-free gum after meals.** Chewing gets your saliva flowing, which washes away food and bacteria.
5. **Breathe through your nose.** Mouth breathing, especially at night, dries your mouth out and makes morning breath worse.
6. **Cut back on smoking, alcohol, and coffee.** All three dry your mouth and feed odour.
7. **See a dentist if it does not go away.** Bad breath that lasts can be a sign of gum disease or a cavity that needs treatment.

Start with the first three. For many people, tongue cleaning alone makes a noticeable difference within days.

## Does mouthwash fix bad breath?

Mostly, it covers it up. Many mouthwashes mask the smell for an hour or two, and some alcohol-based ones can even dry your mouth out. They are fine as an extra, but they do not fix the cause.

## Can probiotics help with bad breath?

This is where oral probiotics come in. The idea is to add helpful bacteria to your mouth so the smelly ones have less room to grow.

- **Some small studies** have found that certain probiotic strains can reduce bad breath for a while.
- **The research is still limited.** Studies are small, use different strains, and the benefits often fade when people stop.
- **Probiotics work best on top of good habits**, not instead of them.
