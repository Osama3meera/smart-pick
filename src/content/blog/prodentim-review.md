## What is inside ProDentim?

Each tablet is listed as containing **3.5 billion CFU** (colony-forming units) of probiotic bacteria, plus a few supporting ingredients:

- **Lactobacillus paracasei**
- **Bifidobacterium lactis BL-04**
- **Lactobacillus reuteri**
- **Inulin**, a plant fibre that feeds good bacteria
- **Malic acid** and **tricalcium phosphate**

The idea is simple: your mouth has its own community of bacteria, and adding helpful strains may help keep the harmful ones in check.

## What does the science say?

Here is the honest part.

- **Oral probiotics are a real area of research.** Some studies on strains like *Lactobacillus reuteri* have found modest improvements in gum health and plaque in people with gingivitis.
- **Results are mixed and usually small.** Studies use different strains and doses, and the effects tend to fade when people stop taking them.
- **No study has tested ProDentim itself**, as far as we could find. The research is on individual strains, not this exact formula.
- **Some marketing claims go much further than the evidence**, such as supporting your sinuses and ear canals. We would not buy it for those.
- Like all supplements, it has not been evaluated by the FDA and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

So ProDentim is best seen as an easy extra for your daily routine, not a replacement for brushing, flossing, or seeing a dentist.

## 7 free habits for healthier teeth and gums

Whether or not you try ProDentim, these habits make the biggest difference:

1. **Brush twice a day for two minutes.** Use a fluoride toothpaste and a soft brush, and do not scrub hard.
2. **Spit, do not rinse.** After brushing, skip the water rinse so the fluoride keeps protecting your teeth.
3. **Clean between your teeth daily.** Floss or interdental brushes reach where your toothbrush cannot.
4. **Cut how often you eat sugar.** It is the number of sugary snacks per day that matters most, not just the amount.
5. **Drink water after meals.** It helps wash away food and keeps your mouth from drying out.
6. **Replace your toothbrush every three months.** Worn bristles clean much less well.
7. **See your dentist regularly.** Small problems are cheaper and easier to fix early.
